How to Play

  1. Take turns. On your turn, select any uncolored region, then pick a color (or pick the color first, then click the region). Keys 1–4 pick colors.
  2. No conflicts. A region may not share a color with any neighbor that is already colored.
  3. Scoring. Valid move +10 and the turn passes. Invalid attempt −5, the move is rejected and it stays your turn.
  4. Permanent ownership. A successfully colored region is locked to its owner. Nobody — including the owner — can recolor or clear it.
  5. Turn timer. If enabled and it runs out, the turn passes with no score change.
  6. Undo. Only the latest valid move can be taken back, before the opponent acts. The board, scores and turn are restored exactly.
  7. Game over. When every region is colored, or no uncolored region has a legal color left. Highest score wins; equal scores are a draw.
  8. AI tools. Hints, Solve and Show Steps run a CSP solver on a copy of the board. They respect locked regions and never award points.

All maps are simplified schematic maps; their adjacency lists are checked against the drawn borders.
